How it works

Three steps. The hardest one is remembering your salary.

  1. 1

    Enter your salary

    In VND or USD. Set your hours, your workdays (any of the seven), your lunch break, and whether Vietnamese public holidays count.

  2. 2

    Watch it go up

    A counter that ticks every second: today, this week, this month. It takes lunch, evenings and weekends off too.

  3. 3

    Time it, get a receipt

    Toilet, meeting, scrolling, waiting for the boss. Tap End for a receipt, a red rubber stamp and a shower of coins.

Screenshots show the Vietnamese interface; the app is fully in English too.

Money is just pho with extra steps

Tap the counter to change units. One hour-long meeting at 29.7 đồng a second is 106,920 đồng, which is:

  • 2.1bowls of pho₫50,000 a bowl
  • 2.4cups of milk tea₫45,000 a cup
  • 0.19blind boxes₫550,000 a box
  • 0.43%of an iPhone₫25,000,000 each

Prices in the app are rounded for the joke, not market prices. USD salaries use dollar prices.

Quick answers

How much is a second worth?

Your monthly salary divided by the seconds you're actually paid for that month. Fewer workdays means each second is worth more. With holidays on, holidays don't count.

Why did the counter stop?

Lunch, after hours, the weekend or a public holiday. Off the clock, the timer runs but the money doesn't.

Does it cost anything?

It's free. No ads, no in-app purchases, no account.

Is this my real payroll?

No. The number is entertainment, not payroll or financial advice.
